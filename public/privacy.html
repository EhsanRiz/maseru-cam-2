Privacy Policy

4D Traffic Bot (traffic.4dcs.co.za and the 4D Traffic Bot mobile app)
Effective date: 3 November 2026

The short version
  • We only record your location when you are inside the Maseru Bridge border area, and only if you turn on Crossing Mode.
  • Location data from a crossing is linked to a random trip ID, not to your name or account.
  • Raw location points are deleted within 48 hours. We keep only crossing times and anonymous statistics.
  • We never sell your personal information or share your individual location with anyone, including border authorities.
  • You can stop location sharing at any time and ask us to delete your account.

1. Who we are

4D Traffic Bot is operated by 4D Climate Solutions, a company registered in the Kingdom of Lesotho ("4DCS", "we", "us"). We are responsible for the personal information described in this policy.

Contact: info@4dcs.co.za · +266 2232 3442

We process personal information in line with the Lesotho Data Protection Act, 2011. For users in South Africa we also follow the Protection of Personal Information Act, 2013 (POPIA).

2. What the service does

4D Traffic Bot helps travellers and border authorities see how long it takes to cross the Maseru Bridge border post between Lesotho and South Africa, in both directions. We combine anonymous crossing times from app users, wait-time reports from travellers and historical patterns to estimate current waiting times.

3. Information we collect

3.1 Account information

When you register: your name, mobile number and country code, country of residence, email address (optional) and a PIN, which we store only in hashed form. We use this to run your account, secure it and let you reset your password.

3.2 Location data (Crossing Mode only)

If you turn on Crossing Mode and give location permission, the app uses your device's location only while you are inside a defined area around the Maseru Bridge border post (roughly 3 km around the bridge). Outside that area, we collect no location data.

  • What: GPS position, speed, accuracy and time, recorded about every 15 to 30 seconds while you are inside the border area, plus the times you enter and leave zones such as the queue, each border post and the bridge.
  • Background use: on the mobile app, if you allow it, location is collected in the background so that a crossing is measured even when the app is closed. The phone's own geofencing wakes the app only when you enter the border area. On the website, location is used only while the page is open.
  • How it is stored: each crossing gets a new random trip ID. Trip data is not linked to your name, phone number or account.
  • Why: to work out live queue length and speed, and how long a crossing takes, in each direction.

Location sharing is optional. You can use the rest of the service without it, and you can turn it off at any time in the app or in your phone's settings.

3.3 Wait-time and window reports

When you submit a report, we store the direction (LS→SA or SA→LS), the part of the crossing it refers to (for example LS departure or SA arrival), the times or queue details you enter, and when you submitted it. We use these reports to improve the wait-time estimate shown to everyone.

3.4 Chat and feedback

If you use the chat assistant, we store your questions and the answers so we can show your history and improve the service. Your messages are sent to an AI provider (Google Gemini) to generate answers. Please do not include sensitive personal information in chat. We also store feedback and reactions you give.

3.5 Usage and technical information

  • Basic activity in the service (for example when you last used it), so we can count active users.
  • Your IP address and the time you accepted our terms, as a record of consent.
  • Website analytics through Google Analytics (pages viewed, device and browser type, approximate region).

3.6 Border camera images

The website may show or analyse images from a public traffic camera at the border. These images show the road and vehicles from a distance. We do not use them to identify individuals or read number plates.

4. How we use information

  • To calculate and publish live and historical crossing times for each direction.
  • To provide forecasts and aggregated statistics (for example average waits by hour and day) to the public and to border and transport authorities. These are always anonymous and aggregated. We never share an individual's trips or location.
  • To run your account, answer your questions and send alerts you ask for.
  • To detect abuse, false reports and security problems.
  • To improve the service.

We rely on your consent for location data and on our legitimate interest in running a reliable, secure service for everything else. You can withdraw consent at any time.

6. Who we share information with

We do not sell personal information. We share it only with service providers that help us run the service, under agreements that require them to protect it:

  • Supabase: database and file storage.
  • Render: application hosting.
  • Google: Gemini (chat assistant and image analysis), Google Analytics, and Google Play services on Android.

These providers may store data outside Lesotho and South Africa, for example in the European Union or the United States. When they do, we rely on their contractual and security commitments to protect it.

We may also disclose information if the law requires it, for example under a valid court order. Where the law allows, we will tell you.

7. Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). PINs are hashed, and access to databases is restricted to authorised staff. Location trips use random IDs so that a leaked trip record does not reveal who made it. No system is completely secure, but we work to protect your information and will notify affected users and the regulator of a serious breach as the law requires.

8. Your rights

You may:

  • ask what personal information we hold about you and get a copy of it;
  • ask us to correct or delete it;
  • withdraw consent to location sharing (turn off Crossing Mode or remove the permission in your phone settings);
  • object to how we use your information;
  • complain to the data protection authority in Lesotho or, in South Africa, to the Information Regulator (inforegulator.org.za).

To use any of these rights, or to delete your account, email info@4dcs.co.za with the subject "Privacy request". We will reply within 30 days.

9. Children

The service is not intended for children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the service changes. We will post the new version here with a new effective date, and tell you in the app before any material change takes effect, especially changes to how we use location data.

Before you start

Crossing Mode measures how long your crossing takes so everyone can see live waiting times.

  • Your location is sent only while you are inside the border area (about 3 km around the bridge).
  • Each crossing gets a random ID, not linked to your name or account.
  • Raw locations are deleted within 48 hours. Only the crossing time is kept.
  • On this website, tracking only works while this page is open.

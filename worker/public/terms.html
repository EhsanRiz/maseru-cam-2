2. What Tsela does

Tsela shows estimated times to cross the Maseru Bridge border between Lesotho and South Africa, in both directions, and a forecast of quieter times. Estimates are worked out from crossings measured by travellers who choose to share their location inside the border area, and from wait-time reports they send.

Tsela is not run by, or connected with, the governments, border authorities, police or revenue services of Lesotho or South Africa.

3. Estimates are not guarantees

Crossing times change quickly and depend on how many travellers are sharing data. Tsela's figures may be late, incomplete or wrong, and the service may sometimes be unavailable. Always follow official instructions, signs and border staff. Plan your journey, including any appointments or connections, with this in mind.

4. Safety

  • Do not handle your phone while driving. Start Tsela before you drive, use a hands-free mount, or ask a passenger.
  • Obey road rules and the instructions of officials at all times. Tsela's prompts, such as the window report pop-up, can wait until you are safely stopped.
  • Check whether phones may be used in the border buildings, and follow the rules there.

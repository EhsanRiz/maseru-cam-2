Privacy Policy

Maseru Border Times (traffic.4dcs.co.za and the Maseru Border Times mobile app)
Effective date: 7 November 2026

The short version
  • We only use your location when you are inside the Maseru Bridge border area, and only after you tap Start.
  • Each crossing gets a random trip ID, not linked to your name or phone number.
  • Raw location points are deleted within 48 hours. For the public times we keep only anonymous crossing times.
  • If you choose, we save your own journeys for 7, 30, 60 or 90 days, then delete them automatically.
  • We never sell your information or share your individual location with anyone, including border authorities.

1. Who we are

Maseru Border Times is operated by 4D Climate Solutions (Pty) Ltd, a company registered in the Kingdom of Lesotho ("4DCS", "we", "us"). We are responsible for the personal information described in this policy.

We process personal information in line with the Lesotho Data Protection Act, 2011. For users in South Africa we also follow the Protection of Personal Information Act, 2013 (POPIA).

2. What the service does

Maseru Border Times shows how long it takes to cross the Maseru Bridge border post between Lesotho and South Africa, in both directions. Times come from travellers' phones measuring their own crossings, and from wait-time reports travellers send. We do not use cameras.

3. Information we collect

3.1 Location data while you cross

When you tap Start and allow location access, the app uses your device's location only while you are inside a defined area around the Maseru Bridge border post (roughly 3 km around the bridge). Outside that area, nothing is sent to us.

  • What: GPS position, speed, accuracy and time, recorded every few seconds while you are inside the border area.
  • Background use (mobile app only): if you allow it, the app collects location in the background so that a crossing is measured even when the app is closed. The phone's own geofencing wakes the app only when you enter the border area. On the website, location is used only while the page is open.
  • How it is stored: each crossing gets a new random trip ID. We do not ask for or store your name, phone number or email address.
  • Why: to work out live queue length and speed, and how long a crossing takes in each direction.
  • Your route map: the map of your route is drawn and kept on your phone only. It is not sent to us. You can clear it at any time. Map images are loaded from OpenStreetMap, which sees the map area being viewed but not your location history.

Location sharing is optional. You can see the live times without it, and you can stop at any time with the Stop button or in your phone's settings.

3.2 Your saved journeys (optional)

If you turn on Save my journeys, we keep a record of each completed crossing: date and time, direction, whether you crossed by vehicle or on foot, total time and the time spent in each part (queue, each border post, the bridge). We do not keep your route.

  • Your journeys are stored under a private history key created on your phone. We store only a scrambled (hashed) form of the key, not your name or number.
  • You choose how long journeys are kept: 7, 30, 60 or 90 days. Each journey is deleted automatically when that period ends. If you shorten the period, older journeys are deleted straight away.
  • You can delete one journey, or all of them, at any time in the app.
  • Anyone who has your history key can see your saved journeys, so keep it private.

The public times use a separate, anonymous copy of each crossing. The link between a crossing and your history is removed as soon as the crossing is processed.

3.3 Wait-time and window reports

When you send a report, we store the direction, which border window it refers to, the numbers you enter (windows open, people ahead, minutes waited) and when you sent it. Reports are anonymous.

3.4 Technical information

Our hosting provider, Cloudflare, processes your IP address and basic request details to deliver the service and protect it from abuse. We use these only for security and to limit misuse; we do not use them to identify you. Settings such as your consent and history key are stored on your own device.

4. How we use information

  • To calculate and publish live and typical crossing times for each direction.
  • To provide anonymous, aggregated statistics (for example average waits by hour and day) to the public and to border and transport authorities. We never share an individual's crossings or location.
  • To show you your own saved journeys, if you turned that on.
  • To detect abuse, false reports and security problems, and to improve the service.

We rely on your consent for location data and saved journeys, and on our legitimate interest in running a reliable, secure service for everything else. You can withdraw consent at any time.

6. Who we share information with

We do not sell personal information. The service runs on Cloudflare (hosting, database and security), which processes data on our behalf under contractual and security commitments. Cloudflare may process data outside Lesotho and South Africa.

We may also disclose information if the law requires it, for example under a valid court order. Where the law allows, we will tell you.

7. Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Trip keys and history keys are stored only in hashed form, and access to the database is restricted to authorised staff. Crossings use random IDs so that a leaked record does not reveal who made it. No system is completely secure, but we work to protect your information and will notify affected users and the regulator of a serious breach as the law requires.

8. Your rights

You may:

  • see, and delete, your saved journeys at any time in the app;
  • ask what personal information we hold about you and get a copy of it;
  • ask us to correct or delete it;
  • withdraw consent to location sharing (tap Stop, or remove the permission in your phone settings);
  • object to how we use your information;
  • complain to the data protection authority in Lesotho or, in South Africa, to the Information Regulator (inforegulator.org.za).

Because we do not know who you are, please include your history key if your request is about saved journeys. Email info@4dcs.co.za with the subject "Privacy request". We will reply within 30 days.

9. Children

The service is not intended for children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the service changes. We will post the new version here with a new effective date, and tell you in the app before any material change takes effect, especially changes to how we use location data.

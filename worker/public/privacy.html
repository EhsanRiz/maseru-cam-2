Privacy Policy

Tsela: Maseru Bridge crossing times (traffic.4dcs.co.za and the Tsela mobile app)
Effective date: 10 November 2026

The short version
  • We only use your location when you are inside the Maseru Bridge border area, and only after you tap Start.
  • The public crossing times are anonymous. Raw location points are deleted within 48 hours.
  • An account is optional. If you create one, we keep your email, and your name and phone number if you add them.
  • Signed-in users can save their crossings and route maps for 7, 30, 60 or 90 days. They are then deleted automatically.
  • We never sell your information or share your individual location with anyone, including border authorities.

1. Who we are

Tsela is operated by 4D Climate Solutions (Pty) Ltd, a company registered in the Kingdom of Lesotho ("4DCS", "we", "us"). We are responsible for the personal information described in this policy.

We process personal information in line with the Lesotho Data Protection Act, 2011. For users in South Africa we also follow the Protection of Personal Information Act, 2013 (POPIA).

2. What the service does

Tsela shows how long it takes to cross the Maseru Bridge border post between Lesotho and South Africa, in both directions. Times come from travellers' phones measuring their own crossings, and from wait-time reports travellers send. We do not use cameras.

3. Information we collect

3.1 Location data while you cross

When you tap Start and allow location access, the app uses your device's location only while you are inside a defined area around the Maseru Bridge border post (roughly 3 km around the bridge). Outside that area, nothing is sent to us.

  • What: GPS position, speed, accuracy and time, recorded every few seconds while you are inside the border area.
  • Background use (mobile app only): if you allow it, the app collects location in the background so that a crossing is measured even when the app is closed. The phone's own geofencing wakes the app only when you enter the border area. On the website, location is used only while the page is open.
  • For the public times: each crossing gets a new random trip ID and is not linked to your account. Raw points are deleted within 48 hours; only the anonymous crossing times are kept.
  • The map on the Dashboard is drawn and kept on your phone. Map images are loaded from OpenStreetMap, which sees the map area being viewed but not your location history.

Location sharing is optional. You can see the live times without it, and stop at any time with the Stop button or in your phone's settings.

3.2 Your account (optional)

You can use Tsela without an account. If you create one, we keep:

  • your email address, used to sign in and to send you your PIN;
  • your name and phone number, only if you add them in Settings;
  • your preferences (for example how you usually cross and how long to keep journeys);
  • a PIN, stored only in scrambled (salted and hashed) form so that nobody, including us, can read it;
  • sign-in records: when you last signed in and a hashed session code that keeps you signed in on a device for 7 days.

3.3 Your saved journeys and route maps (optional)

If you are signed in and Save my crossings is on, we keep a record of each completed crossing: date and time, direction, whether you crossed by vehicle or on foot, total time, the time spent in each part (queue, each border post, the bridge), and a simplified map of your route inside the border area.

  • You choose how long journeys are kept: 7, 30, 60 or 90 days. Each journey and its route map are deleted automatically when that period ends. If you shorten the period, older journeys are deleted straight away.
  • You can turn saving off, delete one journey, or delete all of them at any time in the app.
  • Only you can see your saved journeys.

The public times use a separate, anonymous copy of each crossing. The link between that copy and your account is removed as soon as the crossing is processed.

3.4 Wait-time and window reports

When you send a report, we store the direction, which border window it refers to, the numbers you enter (windows open, people ahead, minutes waited) and when you sent it. Reports are anonymous.

3.5 Technical information

Our hosting provider, Cloudflare, processes your IP address and basic request details to deliver the service and protect it from abuse. We use these only for security and to limit misuse. Your sign-in session and settings are also stored on your own device.

4. How we use information

  • To calculate and publish live and typical crossing times for each direction.
  • To provide anonymous, aggregated statistics (for example average waits by hour and day) to the public and to border and transport authorities. We never share an individual's crossings, routes or location.
  • To run your account: sign you in, email you your PIN, and show your saved journeys.
  • To detect abuse, false reports and security problems, and to improve the service.

We rely on your consent for location data, your account and saved journeys, and on our legitimate interest in running a reliable, secure service for everything else. You can withdraw consent at any time.

6. Who we share information with

We do not sell personal information. We use these service providers, which process data on our behalf under contractual and security commitments:

  • Cloudflare: hosting, database and security.
  • Resend: sending sign-in PIN emails (receives your email address and the email content).
  • OpenStreetMap: map images (receives the map area being viewed, not your account or history).

These providers may process data outside Lesotho and South Africa. We may also disclose information if the law requires it, for example under a valid court order. Where the law allows, we will tell you.

7. Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). PINs, session codes and trip keys are stored only in hashed form. Repeated wrong PINs lock sign-in for 15 minutes, and access to the database is restricted to authorised staff. No system is completely secure, but we work to protect your information and will notify affected users and the regulator of a serious breach as the law requires.

8. Your rights

You may:

  • see, download and delete your data in the app (Settings → Your data), including deleting your whole account;
  • correct your name and phone number in Settings → Profile;
  • withdraw consent to location sharing (tap Stop, or remove the permission in your phone settings) and turn off saving journeys;
  • ask us what personal information we hold about you, or object to how we use it;
  • complain to the data protection authority in Lesotho or, in South Africa, to the Information Regulator (inforegulator.org.za).

For anything you cannot do in the app, email info@4dcs.co.za with the subject "Privacy request" from the email address on your account. We will reply within 30 days.

9. Children

The service is not intended for children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the service changes. We will post the new version here with a new effective date, and tell you in the app before any material change takes effect, especially changes to how we use location data.

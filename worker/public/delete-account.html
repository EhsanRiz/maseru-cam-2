Delete your Tsela account and data

Tsela: Maseru Bridge crossing times, by 4D Climate Solutions (Pty) Ltd

Option 1: delete it yourself (immediate)

This works in the Tsela app and on the website.

  1. Open the Tsela app, or go to traffic.4dcs.co.za, and sign in with your email and PIN.
  2. Open the menu (☰) and choose Settings, then the Your data tab.
  3. Tap Delete my account and confirm.

Forgotten your PIN? On the sign-in screen tap Forgot PIN and we will email you a new one.

To delete only your saved journeys and route maps but keep your account, choose Delete all my journeys on the same tab instead.

Option 2: ask us to delete it

Email info@4dcs.co.za with the subject "Delete my Tsela account", from the email address you use to sign in to Tsela. We will delete the account within 30 days and confirm by email.

What is deleted

  • Your account: email address, name, phone number, preferences and PIN.
  • All your saved journeys and their route maps.
  • All your sign-in sessions, on every device.

What is kept

  • Anonymous crossing times and window reports that were already part of the public crossing estimates. They are not linked to you or your account.
  • Raw location points from a crossing are deleted automatically within 48 hours, whether or not you delete your account.

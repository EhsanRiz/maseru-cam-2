Before you start

Tsela measures how long your crossing takes so everyone can see live waiting times.

  • Your location is used only while you are inside the border area (about 3 km around the bridge).
  • The public times are anonymous: each crossing gets a random ID. Raw locations are deleted within 48 hours.
  • If you sign in, your crossings and their route maps are saved to your account for the period you choose (7, 30, 60 or 90 days). You can turn this off in Settings.
  • On this website, tracking only works while this page is open. The Android app keeps going with the screen off.
  • While you cross, Android shows a notification. Tracking continues with the screen off and stops when you tap Stop or leave the border area.
